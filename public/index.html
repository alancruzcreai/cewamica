i. about

A space where internal
processes take form.

Cewámica is not a brand. It is a project rooted in ancestral ways of working with clay — gathering it, preparing it, transforming it in conversation with the place it comes from.

That foundation meets a contemporary sensibility. Each piece is built from an intuitive process, guided by what emerges, not by predefined forms. We work with wild clay, fire, and presence.

The artist

Salomé Le Lay

— present, without performing.

Five years ago, my life turned. I came from design and creative direction, and from a path marked by long periods of gestation and transformation. I entered service work and began holding space for people in transition.

Clay has remained my personal anchor: a way to slow down, integrate, and return to the body. Barro Contigo comes from there.

iv. the medium

From the earth
to the fire.

Six gestures, in order. Each one shapes the next. There is no rushing the medium — only meeting it.

  1. 01

    Gather

    Walk to the place. Read the soil. Take only what is asked for.

  2. 02

    Prepare

    Rest the clay with aloe and water. Strain. Let the body settle.

  3. 03

    Explore

    What emerges leads. The hand listens before it decides.

  4. 04

    Build

    Coil and pinch. Walk around the piece. Stay with it as it takes shape.

  5. 05

    Burnish

    Stone polish over hours. The surface remembers every pass of the hand.

  6. 06

    Fire

    Pit fire — wood, smoke, ash. A final alchemy where the piece is released.
